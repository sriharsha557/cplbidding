## ✅ Bidding Rules

1. **Base Price**: Minimum bid amount (set per player)
2. **Bid Increments**: Minimum 5 tokens
3. **Time Limit**: 60 seconds per player
4. **Validation**: System checks affordability automatically

### Can I Bid? Checklist
- ✅ Enough total tokens remaining?
- ✅ Enough category budget remaining?
- ✅ Squad not full (< 15 players)?
- ✅ Role slots available?

---

## 🎲 Strategy Tips

### DO ✅
- Plan your budget across all categories
- Save tokens for later phases
- Track competitors' budgets
- Aim for balanced squad composition

### DON'T ❌
- Spend all budget on one category
- Ignore minimum requirements
- Bid beyond your category budget
- Wait too long to fill critical roles

## 🚨 Common Mistakes

1. **Overspending Early**: Spending 350 tokens on first 2 batsmen
2. **Ignoring Categories**: Forgetting to save for wicketkeepers
3. **Missing Minimums**: Not meeting 4-player minimum in a category
4. **No Buffer**: Using all 1,200 tokens with no flexibility

## 📱 During the Auction

### What You'll See
- Current player details (photo, name, role, base price)
- Your team's remaining budget
- Your category budget for current player
- Real-time affordability indicator

### What You'll Do
1. Watch the current player
2. Decide your maximum bid
3. Call out your bid verbally
4. Admin records the winning bid
5. System validates and updates

## 🏆 Winning Formula

- **Timing**: Know when to bid aggressively vs. conservatively
